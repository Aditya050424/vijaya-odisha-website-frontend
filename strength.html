OUR SATISFACTION

Our Satisfaction

On the backdrop of last few years of its operation, VIJAYA has today occupied a unique position in the matrix of state’s health and socio- economic empowerment programmes.

From capacity building of communities especially underprivileged, unreached and marginalized ones to consultancy, policy advocacy, reforms and health networking, VIJAYA has emerged out as a winning team.

With a vision of social equity, sustainable development goal, common hood and empathy towards each other, VIJAYA has marched ahead in its victorious journey against social discrimination, gender bias, ill health, illiteracy and disability and poverty.
