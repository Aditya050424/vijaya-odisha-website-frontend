02
PROJECT LOCATION

Puri

The organisation operates activities for rehabilitation, empowerment and support of women and disadvantaged people.

The following Special activities at Swadhar Home have been undertaken

  • Providing rehabilitation and institutional support to distressed women.
  • Facilitating marriages and self and wage employment.
  • Providing vocational training and skill development.
  • Providing support for education and rehabilitation.
  • Supporting health care and medical needs.
SWADHAR HOME

Swadhar Home Activities

All abled and capable inmates are currently engaged in different in house activities like operating omfed milk distribution booth, cereal making, temple centric articles making and wheat and dry grinding of spices and dough and idle and Dosa and also paper conversation activities.

03
PROJECT LOCATION

Bhadrak

SPECIAL SCHOOL

A Special Residential School For Deaf, Dumb & Blind Girls.

Special activities

  • Enabled nine disadvantaged girls for going for higher education by admitting them at college level education.
  • Helped in the Restoration of better eyesight to Twenty-two blind girls through minor and major operations at Rotary eye hospital at Cuttack, Aurobindo Eye Hospital at Pondicherry and Sankar Netralay at Chennai in different time intervals as referred. cornea grafting of four girls at Sankar Netralaya at Chennai have been done.
  • Helped in the empowerment of hearing ability for hearing impaired girls through intensive checkups supplemented by supply of hearing aids and Sign Language Training.
  • Imparted Vocational training through KVIC, Govt. of India for self employment.
  • Sign language training in collaboration with National Training Centre for the Teachers of Deaf at Janla.

WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Women Empowerment Centre at Satyabhamapur

A nodal centre with a local co-coordinator has been established with 250 women from a cluster of 9 villages having registered with the centers expressing their readiness & willingness for active participation in different development Health empowerment and awareness programmes.

More than 120 of them have already been trained in collaboration with KVIC of Govt. of India.
