PROJECT LOCATION

Bhadrak

🎓
SPECIAL RESIDENTIAL SCHOOL

Supporting girls through education & empowerment.

A special residential school for deaf, dumb and blind girls, with initiatives focused on education, healthcare, hearing support, vocational training and independence.

🎓
Higher Education

Enabled nine disadvantaged girls to pursue higher education at college level.

◉
Eye Care

Supported restoration of better eyesight through medical operations.

◇
Hearing Support

Checkups, hearing aids and sign language training.

✦
Vocational Training

Vocational training through KVIC for self employment.

+

Sign language training is also conducted in collaboration with the National Training Centre for the Teachers of Deaf at Janla.
