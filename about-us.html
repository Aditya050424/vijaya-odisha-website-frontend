ABOUT VIJAYA

Working for a better tomorrow.

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th October 1996 bearing Registration No- “20154/118-1996-97”.

The Registered office of the organization is located at 417, Sahid Nagar, Bhubaneswar. The organization was registered under Foreign Contribution (Registration Act) 1976 on 14th July, 2000 and also registered under U/S 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income Tax Act 1961.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision & Mission.

Guided by our values, we work towards creating a more inclusive, empowered and sustainable society.

01 OUR VISION
◉

Vision

VIJAYA aspires a just society, where people irrespective of cast & creed, rich & poor, powerful & powerless, male & female live at par with each other following the values of sustainable development, global common hood and empathy towards each others.

A JUST SOCIETY ↗
02 OUR MISSION
✦

Mission

Initiate people oriented development process in which men and women of poverty groups (Backward, Tribal and Costal) shall come together to change their powerlessness situation and enhance their socio economic health and educational standard and carry on the process even after the NGO withdraws.

PEOPLE-CENTRED DEVELOPMENT ↗
